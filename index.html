About us

Our passsions drive us

We are very passionate about two things: Serving humanity and applying technology to small business problems. Our business model allows us to do both.

Casas por Cristo

We began building homes along the U.S. / México border in 1993, first in Ciudad Juárez. We have since expanded to Ciudad Acuña, México; San Raymundo, Guatemala; Santiago, Dominican Republic; and Chinandega, Nicaragua. To date, we have provided over 5,000 families with homes. Learn more

Villa Esperanza

Villa Esperanza was born out of the dream to rescue and redeem girls living in and around Nicaragua's largest open-air garbage dump. Over ten years later, the Villa has expanded to serving young boys, families and the entire community as well. Learn more

City Lights Tulsa

Night Light is a weekly outreach event that builds relationships between Tulsa's homeless population, lower income families, and the surrounding community. Learn more
